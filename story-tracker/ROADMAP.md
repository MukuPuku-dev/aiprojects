# Prompt tracker

The app is for keeping a project's video prompts organized by date, whether a prompt was generated here or written elsewhere.

## Main workflow

1. Add or choose a project.
2. Optionally add project/story, character, visual-style, and requirement notes under **Project continuity guidance**.
3. Generate one prompt for a selected date, or paste and save a prompt made elsewhere.
4. Review, copy, edit, or delete saved prompts in the dated history.

Prompt generation is an explicit action and makes one AI request. It uses the project's guidance and up to the 10 most recent saved prompts to support continuity and avoid repetition. It does not create an idea, script, or Flow video automatically.

Existing project records are retained. Prompts saved in earlier episodes and inbox notes are shown in the history alongside newly saved prompts.
